How it connects

Design by conversation

“Make it 900 wide, add two drawers and open the doors.”

The assistant uses the same sidebar, right-click menus and windows you do — same checks, same refusals, and every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z. It can also turn the 3D view and show you a picture.

Hardware scouting

“Find a cheaper hinge for these doors and put it in the project.”

With an AI app that can browse, it compares hinges, slides and handles on fit and price, adds the article to your Hardware Library with its supplier link and price, and fits it in the cabinet.

Quotes in Excel

“Make me the quote for this kitchen.”

Panels in m² per material, edge bands in metres, hardware in pieces — at your library prices, waste included. Add your labour time in its own line. A real Excel file with formulas: change a price and the totals follow. Rows without a price are listed, never counted as zero.

Powerful Features

Everything You Need
to Build Beautiful Cabinets

Designed for professional woodworkers and hobbyists alike.

Parametric 2D & 3D Design

Drive the whole cabinet from parameters. Resize, split openings, add dividers, doors and shelves with a live 3D preview.

  • Parametric openings & dividers
  • Real-time 3D preview
  • Undo / redo & project save

Photorealistic Rendering

Turn compositions into client-ready visuals. Real materials and lighting produce studio-quality room renders straight from your design.

  • Studio-quality room renders
  • Real materials & lighting
  • Share visuals with clients

Cabinet Typologies

Not just boxes. Design standard carcasses, diagonal corner units and slanted-top cabinets — each with its own parametric geometry.

  • Standard carcass
  • Diagonal & 90° corner units (angoliera)
  • Slanted-top unit — 5-axis machining

Catalogs & Hardware Library

Editable material and edge-band palettes plus a full hardware library. Assign materials and hardware per panel, per door, per edge.

  • Editable material & edge-band catalogs
  • Hinges, guides, handles, feet, joints
  • Blum Tandem / Movento guides

Cut List & Optimization

Auto-generate detailed cut lists with grain direction and edge-band compensation, so the panel saw gets the real finished sizes.

  • Auto cut-list generation
  • Edge-band size compensation
  • Export to Excel / CSV

Native CNC Export — SCM Maestro

Export ready-to-run programs for SCM Maestro. Drilling, milling, blade cuts and toe-kick pockets emitted as native Maestro operations — as XCS or straight to PGMX via XConverter.

  • SCM Maestro output — XCS & PGMX
  • Milling, blade cuts, toe-kick pockets
  • F6 bottom-face split & DXF / CSV

Quoted Technical Drawings New

One PDF plate per piece, built from the same cut list your saw uses. Main view plus the four edges unfolded around it, contour, thickness and angular dimensions, and every operation listed in a machining table.

  • One plate per piece — views, quotas, machining table
  • Edge banding on the plate: stroke, code & legend
  • Finished or pre-banding sizes, stated on the plate

Inclined Cuts — On or Off the Machine New

Bevels and tilted holes are real 5-axis operations, never a rotated approximation. With a 5-axis head they go straight to Maestro. Without one, declare no 5-axis tool and the same cuts stay in the model, the cut list and the drawings, flagged as off-machine work.

  • Tilted blade cuts & inclined drilling
  • Native Maestro output on 5-axis machines
  • Off-machine cuts documented on the drawings

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before you download.

Which AI can I use with Cabinet Designer?

Any AI app that speaks MCP and runs on the same PC. We tested it with the free ChatGPT desktop app (no subscription; download it separately from OpenAI); it also works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, VS Code, Cursor and LM Studio for fully local models. In Cabinet Designer open the AI assistant menu, tick Enable the AI assistant, then use AI assistant settings to connect your app in one click or create its plugin.

Can the AI change my project or prices without asking?

It works through the normal interface, so every change shows on screen and can be undone. CNC safety confirmations are always yours, and when the AI changes a price the program asks you to confirm it, with No as the default. The assistant is off until you switch it on, and only the AI app you connect can reach it.

What can I do with the free trial?

Design without limits — every typology, the full hardware library, drawers, shelves, edge management and the room composer. The trial shows everything the Professional version does and only meters the production output: 5 SCM Maestro (CNC) exports, 5 cut lists and 5 quoted-drawing PDFs (Cabinet Designer and the Composer together), plus a DEMO watermark on photorealistic renders. In the Professional version they are unlimited.

Which CNC machines are supported?

Native SCM Maestro export in XCS or PGMX (PGMX generated via XConverter): drilling, milling, blade cuts and toe-kick pockets are emitted as real Maestro operations, including the F6 bottom-face split. Neutral DXF and CSV are also produced for other saws and workflows.

What does Cabinet Designer include?

A complete design-to-machine chain, not a drawing tool: parametric carcasses, corner and slanted-top typologies, the hardware and material libraries, edge-banding technology with blank compensation, cut lists, quoted PDF drawings, photorealistic rendering and native SCM Maestro programs (XCS/PGMX) — including 5-axis operations and the F6 bottom-face split. It covers both Cabinet Designer and the room Composer, in nine languages.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), an Intel Core i5 or equivalent, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), a DirectX 11 GPU and 3 GB of free space. See the full list in the Download section.

In which languages is the software available?

Nine, switchable live inside the app: Italian, English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Arabic — with Arabic both apps are laid out from right to left, user manual included. Every language except Arabic also has its own dedicated YouTube channel; the Arabic one is coming.
